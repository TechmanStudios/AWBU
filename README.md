# A World Between Us

Techman Studios is developing an original cinematic fantasy romance about two people whose connection challenges a kingdom's understanding of magic and human worth. This workspace begins with the owner's Master Project Initialization Brief, Version 1.0.

**Development status:** GO. The owner approved the Project Charter and Canon Register on October 13, 2026. Their provisional entries remain provisional. The Series Bible Outline, Art Direction, and shot proposals remain in development.

The brief and subsequent owner decisions establish the creative foundation. Additions remain proposals unless the Canon Register identifies them as established. The latest story addition is that symbols of Resonance survive in architecture and ornament while their meaning has been completely forgotten. No generated visual assets or video clips have been approved or produced here.

## Working rules

Use names such as `AWBU_SH001_startframe_v001_attempt01.png` and `AWBU_SH001_video_v001_attempt01.mp4`. Change the version when the creative specification changes; change the attempt when regenerating the same specification. Keep original downloads and exact submitted prompts together through their manifest references.

The narrative status of a concept and the production status of an asset are separate. A successful render does not establish canon. A selected environment frame can be approved for this shot while the location's history remains provisional.

Record creative approvals in [Decision Log](development/DECISION_LOG.md), then update the Canon Register if the approval affects story or design. Record asset approvals in the relevant manifest. The project repository is [TechmanStudios AWBU](https://github.com/TechmanStudios/AWBU). Use Git for documents, prompts, and manifests; the supplied ignore rules keep large media and credentials out of ordinary source control.
