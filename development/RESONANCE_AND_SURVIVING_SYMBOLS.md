# Resonance and Surviving Symbols

**Status:** GO as a working development approach, approved with the Start Here package on October 13, 2026. The core premise is established. Historical explanations, exact symbol designs, specific locations, and scene details retain their explicitly open choices. Visual design awaits the owner's anime art references.

## The established premise

Contemporary society has completely forgotten Resonance. Yet people who once understood it left symbols in architecture or ornament that preserve clues to its truth. Later generations retained the forms for their beauty without understanding what they expressed.

A building can therefore carry an idea its present occupants cannot read. A carved design may have become a decorative convention. Its survival does not establish ongoing Resonance activity or a secret contemporary group that remembers the truth.

The owner's example from Shangri-La Frontier informs the broad approach of a later society living among remnants of a forgotten past. AWBU develops its own history, symbolism, architecture, and discovery. This premise does not add a technological civilization or import that story's specific lore.

## Proposed history of the loss of meaning

One possible progression begins with people who understand connection-based awakening and express that understanding in shared spaces. As access and social relationships change, the practices and interpretation become less common. Durable buildings remain useful, and craftsmen repeat attractive patterns even when their original function is no longer taught. Institutions eventually describe the motif in decorative or prestige terms. Familiarity with the shape continues after knowledge of its meaning disappears.

This is a candidate process, not a decided timeline. The academy might occupy, preserve, or incorporate older construction; we have not established that its current institution predates the caste system. Nor have we decided whether a deliberate historical intervention accelerated the forgetting.

The missing step still needs development: what specifically interrupted the practice or recognition of Resonance while profound bonds continued to exist? Surviving imagery supplies evidence for rediscovery; it does not resolve that question by itself.

## Proposed original symbol

Explore two distinct curved forms that create a third figure in their shared negative space. Each form remains recognizable. The encoded idea could be that relationship reveals a possibility neither isolated form makes visible.

Neither part should appear to pour energy into the other, rank one above the other, or erase individual identity. The visual principle can extend to multiple forms so it does not imply that only couples can resonate. Exact geometry, cultural name, materials, and meaning require owner selection.

Possible placements include an old academy arch, a worn floor inlay, and a later ornamental copy elsewhere in the city. These are alternatives for exploration, not three required locations. Begin with one selected form and location so references can reproduce it consistently.

## Proposed discovery progression

1. Introduce the symbol as an ordinary part of the environment. No glow, magical activation, knowing reaction, or explanation signals its meaning.
2. Reintroduce it in a different context. The audience may recognize the shape before the protagonists connect the appearances.
3. Let an experience of connection suggest a new way of reading the relationship between its parts. This is an interpretive clue, not an automatic awakening trigger.
4. Seek corroboration through material context, incomplete records, or other evidence that nobody currently understands as Resonance knowledge.
5. Allow later experiences to confirm, challenge, or refine their interpretation after the romance has earned its emotional weight.

Discovery should involve curiosity, mistakes, and both protagonists' contributions. Kael can notice practical construction or repair details; Aurelia can investigate institutional assumptions and records. These roles depend on their eventual approved skills and goals.

## Use in the first shot

Once the symbol and its placement are approved, a quiet architectural appearance could establish it early. A wide establishing shot should still work when viewers miss that detail. A closer later scene can reveal the exact carving.

For now, the paired-curves art proposal is a candidate for this role. It is not a finalized symbol. Shot 001 should not add a glowing emblem or encode an unapproved design into the starting frame. Preserve a simple placement reference and reuse the selected symbol asset rather than asking a video model to invent a new one each time.

## Decisions still open

Choose the original geometry and first placement, decide how the present building inherited it, and identify the first partial insight the protagonists can earn. Decide the historical cause of forgetting separately. Keep the larger truth of Resonance centered on people and their choices.
