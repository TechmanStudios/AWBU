# A World Between Us Series Bible Outline

**Development status:** GO as the working series bible outline, approved by the owner on October 13, 2026. Art direction remains pending.

The foundations follow the Version 1.0 brief and the owner's October 13, 2026 direction on surviving symbols of Resonance. Proposed directions guide development while their explicit choices remain open. Arc stages describe possible progression rather than a locked season or episode order.

## Story and emotional promise

Develop the logline, audience promise, tone, theme, and boundaries. The central dramatic question is whether two people can remain honest with one another when closeness threatens the identities and expectations their society gave them.

Let the romance move the plot through decisions: choosing to return, admitting an error, defending a boundary, sharing a risk, and trusting someone with a truth. Mystery and adventure grow from those choices.

## Characters and relationships

### Aurelia

Preserve exceptional magical ability and independent motivation. Develop an external goal, private need, family obligations, friendships, habits, humor, limits, and choices she would make without Kael present.

Proposed direction: she wants more people to benefit from a public magical institution, but initially mistakes benevolent intent for understanding. A flaw could be her confidence that she knows how to help. Kael can challenge that assumption; responsibility for changing it remains hers.

Decide her goal before choosing her family conflict. Avoid making every aristocrat cruel or reducing her opposition to a single disapproving parent.

### Kael

Preserve resourcefulness, observation, compassion, dignity, and a sincerely held belief that he has no magic. Develop an occupation, community ties, practical expertise, desired future, vulnerabilities, and a concrete reason he cannot simply leave the kingdom.

Proposed direction: his work keeps an ordinary part of the city functioning. A flaw could be rejecting help because dependence feels dangerous. He should occasionally misjudge Aurelia and have to revise his understanding of her.

Make his competence visible in at least one important action before any magical clue. Do not have awakening erase his work, friendships, or accountability.

### Supporting cast

Develop a friend for each protagonist who has independent loyalties; a magicless community member whose dignity never depends on awakening; a gifted person with limited institutional power; and someone responsible for maintaining the existing order who has a specific stake in it. These are possible functions, not approved characters.

Track who knows each secret, how trust changes, and what every person risks. Communities should contribute to change through their own choices rather than serving as an audience for the protagonists.

## Society and everyday life

Define what the caste system controls: school admission, credentials, work, transport, civic representation, housing, and access to magical services. Choose a few concrete restrictions that recur in scenes instead of explaining a complete legal code at the start.

Show how people live within the system: friendships, markets, care, skilled work, celebrations, compromise, resistance, and disagreement. Class and morality should remain distinct. Privilege shapes opportunities, but it does not determine every person's character.

## Magical systems and Resonance

Document conventional magic's inputs, training, tests, limits, costs, visible language, and uses in ordinary life. Specify what examinations actually measure before defining why they miss Kael's potential.

Keep a separate Resonance section for established principles, candidate rules, observed clues, and unresolved mechanics. Genuine connection awakens existing potential; the precise conditions and effects remain undecided.

The mythology needs an answer to a central tension: people can love deeply even in an unequal society, so social division alone may not explain why Resonance stopped appearing. Possible additional explanations include a missing shared practice or institutions that repeatedly misclassified early signs. Choose carefully later; neither is canon. This preserves the importance of connection while giving the mystery a credible historical mechanism.

Consider reciprocal agency and freely chosen bonds as rules to protect. Avoid making magic a truth detector for romantic compatibility or a reward for constant harmony. A disagreement can deepen understanding without immediately producing power.

## Geography and main locations

An established addition to the world is that old architectural or ornamental symbols preserve clues to Resonance while their meaning has been completely forgotten. The academy is a candidate home for one such symbol; its exact role and history remain proposed. See [Resonance and Surviving Symbols](RESONANCE_AND_SURVIVING_SYMBOLS.md).

| Proposed location | Story function | Visual opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magical academy on raised and suspended terraces | Aurelia's ambition and institutional pressure; controlled access | Open sky, pale stone, interrupted walkways |
| River city beneath the academy | Kael's work, friendships, and social restrictions | Terraced roofs, water, stairs, warm inhabited spaces |
| Shared civic threshold such as a landing or market entrance | Repeated contact across unequal access | Two routes converging on one human-scale space |
| Enchanted forest | Adventure requiring each person's existing strengths | Filtered light, quiet motion, uncertain paths |

Only the broad environmental possibilities are supplied by the brief. Names, layout, history, and specific uses remain proposals. Do not put every location into the teaser.

## Surviving symbols and the mystery

Preserve the difference between what exists and what anyone understands. A decorative form may have been designed by people who knew Resonance, survived changes to a building, and been copied by generations who only valued its beauty. In contemporary society, it carries no recognized Resonance meaning.

Proposed reveal progression: show a form in ordinary surroundings; allow it to recur without explanation; let the protagonists' developing understanding make them notice a relationship between its parts; then use independent historical evidence and their experiences to test that interpretation. Their connection helps them ask a new question. A symbol alone does not awaken Kael or provide an entire magical instruction manual.

One candidate is two distinct curves that create a third figure through the space between them. Its exact geometry, origin, and reading await selection. It should accommodate profound bonds beyond romantic pairs and preserve both individuals' identities.

The survival of stone and ornament helps explain how clues remain. It does not yet explain why the practice disappeared despite deep bonds still existing. The historical mechanism and awakening conditions remain separate open questions.

## Romance progression

1. **Attention:** they notice a quality the other overlooks in themselves. Their first appeal is personal.
2. **Return:** each has a believable reason to see the other again and begins choosing contact.
3. **Ease:** shared humor and ordinary time build familiarity; warmth exists alongside uncertainty.
4. **Friction:** a difference in experience causes a meaningful mistake. They name the harm rather than letting attraction excuse it.
5. **Repair:** an apology changes behavior; trust grows through action.
6. **Vulnerability:** each reveals something that could change how the other sees them.
7. **Commitment:** their bond survives a real consequence, with both retaining agency.
8. **Discovery:** signs of Resonance become understandable only after the relationship has earned emotional weight.

These steps can overlap or recur. The teaser offers attention and possibility, not evidence that the later relationship has already happened.

## Possible major story arcs

| Stage | Emotional movement | Mystery and adventure | Social consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Encounter | Curiosity becomes chosen friendship | Ordinary shared experiences; surviving ornament appears without understood meaning | Visible pressure around access and association |
| Trust under pressure | Affection survives misunderstanding | A search for context; one bounded excursion | Both risk something tied to their separate goals |
| Awakening and rediscovery | Vulnerability becomes commitment | Kael's first earned awakening; symbols and historical evidence begin to acquire meaning | Competing responses to a challenge to accepted tests |
| Shared change | Love becomes a sustained choice | Broader discovery supported by other people | Reform, resistance, reconciliation, and accountability |

The resolution should include concrete improvements for people who remain magicless. The protagonists' strength creates room for change; durable peace needs participation beyond them.
