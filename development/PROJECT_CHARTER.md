# A World Between Us Project Charter

**Studio:** Techman Studios  
**Stage:** Early creative development and preproduction  
**Development status:** GO — approved by the owner on October 13, 2026  
**Foundation:** Owner-supplied Master Project Initialization Brief, Version 1.0  
**Development package date:** October 13, 2026

## Mission

Build an original fantasy romance whose beauty draws audiences into the world, whose characters make them fall in love, and whose story leaves them believing that connection can change the world. Creative enjoyment, emotional truth, and a coherent artistic identity guide the project.

## Story promise

In a kingdom that assigns social standing through recognized magical ability, a gifted aristocratic woman and a supposedly magicless commoner form a relationship that challenges the divide. Their connection gradually awakens dormant potential and opens a path toward rediscovering Resonance.

Resonance emerges through genuine emotional and spiritual connection. Aurelia's love awakens what is already within Kael; it does not grant him power. Other profound bonds may also carry this potential. His discovery exposes a failure of society's understanding and measurement, while human dignity remains independent of magical ability.

The romance draws us close to the characters. The mystery deepens our curiosity about their experiences. Adventure places their developing trust under pressure and widens the consequences of what they discover.

Society has completely forgotten Resonance, yet symbols once expressing its truth survive as architecture and ornament. Modern people appreciate or repeat their forms without understanding their original meaning. Rediscovery can gradually reveal what has been in plain sight throughout the story.

## Creative commitments

- Build affection, humor, disagreement, and trust before Kael's awakening.
- Give Aurelia a goal and a moral journey that remain meaningful outside the romance.
- Let Kael make consequential choices through his existing skills and values.
- Preserve the worth of people whose magic never awakens.
- Reveal Resonance through experiences and clues before explanation.
- Earn social change through collective choices, institutional reform, and reconciliation.
- Develop an original visual identity with reproducible references and deliberate cinematography.

## First deliverable

A proposed 20–30 second teaser in 9:16. Its first production unit is Shot 001, a quiet sunrise establishing shot of the divided kingdom and its magical academy. The shot should communicate wonder, a clear architectural identity, and unequal access without dialogue or complex action.

Success means the owner wants to spend more time in this world; the visual divide reads on a phone; the architecture remains stable during movement; and approved references, exact prompts, generation settings, and review outcomes are preserved.

## Roles and approval

The owner retains final creative approval over major story, character, and visual decisions. The development partner proposes, writes, checks continuity, and organizes production. Drafts and experiments remain provisional until their relevant approval is recorded.

## Immediate scope

The Start Here development package is approved for use as the working plan, with art direction pending. The owner has created a Google Flow project and is gathering anime art examples. Wait for those references before continuing Shot 001 asset development. Then develop the original visual direction with the owner, revise the visual prompts, select an experimental frame, and animate and review the shot. Explicitly unresolved story and design choices remain open.

Season scripts, definitive magic rules, final character designs, new paid services, and publication decisions remain future work.
