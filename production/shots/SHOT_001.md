# A World Between Us Shot 001 Specification

**Title:** The Divided Kingdom  
**Status:** Proposed direction awaiting owner approval  
**Edit duration:** 6 seconds  
**Generation target:** 6 seconds, 9:16, one continuous shot  
**Narrative scope:** Establishing atmosphere and visible social distance; no protagonist reveal or Resonance demonstration

## Composition

A distant sunrise view of a river kingdom. A stone balustrade or roof edge anchors the lower foreground. In the middle distance, terraced streets and clay roofs descend toward a river bend. Above them, a pale-stone academy occupies high cliffs, with a distinctive suspended stone ring and a few floating terraces.

Keep the academy in the upper half and the inhabited city clearly visible below. The river leads the eye upward. Short elevated routes and long ground-level stairways suggest unequal access. Both areas receive warm light; the lower city has color, texture, and care.

The owner has established that misunderstood symbols of Resonance survive in architecture or ornament. An exact symbol and its placement have not yet been approved. The paired arches in the draft prompt are an architectural proposal, not a finalized encoded symbol. Select the symbol design separately before using it as a continuity clue; do not have it glow or activate in this establishing shot.

## Motion and light

Use a slow forward camera move with slight rightward parallax. Foreground stone shifts more than the distant academy. Mist drifts gently and the river carries soft reflections. Architecture stays still; floating terraces do not bob or rotate.

Maintain a single sunrise direction and stable exposure. Restrained violet architectural light may be present from the first frame. It does not intensify into a magical event. No people, crowds, creatures, dialogue, text, or complex action are needed.

## Draft starting frame prompt

Create an original cinematic anime environment keyframe, composed vertically in 9:16. At sunrise, a pale-stone magical academy rises on high cliffs above a beautiful inhabited river city. Give the academy a distinctive suspended stone ring, paired curved arches, and a small number of floating stone terraces. Below, clay rooftops, carefully maintained stepped streets, gardens, and long stairways descend toward a gently curving river. Elevated routes above and longer stairs below subtly suggest unequal access. The same warm sunrise illuminates both parts of the kingdom. View the scene from a distant stone overlook, with a small foreground balustrade and ample atmospheric depth. Keep the main academy tower, suspended ring, and river bend clearly readable. Detailed painted backgrounds, clear anime shapes, refined cel shading, restrained violet architectural light, amber dawn, river blue, natural material texture. Empty foreground and unoccupied visible streets; no characters, creatures, signs, writing, logos, or particle storm. Architecture is coherent, graceful, and original. This is a quiet establishing image, not a magical battle.

## Draft motion prompt

Animate the supplied environment start frame as one continuous quiet establishing shot. Move the camera slowly forward with a slight rightward drift, producing gentle parallax between the foreground stone, the lower city, and the distant academy. Preserve the skyline, tower, suspended ring, terrace positions, river bend, lighting direction, palette, and painted anime style of the reference. Keep all architecture fixed and structurally coherent. Only thin morning mist and soft river reflections move subtly. Maintain the warm sunrise throughout. No cuts, orbit, sudden zoom, new landmarks, people, creatures, text, or expanding magical effects. Ambient wind and distant water only; no speech or music.

Set aspect ratio and duration in the interface; prompt wording alone does not establish these settings. If the model creates unwanted sound, mute it in the edit and build the intended ambience separately.

## Acceptance criteria

- Academy, city, and the distance between their access routes read at phone size.
- Both parts of the city feel like places people could value and inhabit.
- Tower, ring, terraces, and river retain identity through the shot.
- Camera motion feels slow and intentional, with no surprise cut or orbit.
- Lighting and anime treatment remain stable; no bending architecture, flicker, or intrusive generated text.
- Export has the intended portrait dimensions and six-second edit duration without unintended bars or crop loss.
- Manifest links the exact input, submitted prompt, settings, original output, review, and any explicit owner approval.

Change one meaningful variable per retry. If forward motion repeatedly distorts the architecture, reduce it to a near-still camera with atmospheric movement and review whether that still satisfies the establishing-shot intent.
