# A World Between Us Teaser Proposal

**Status:** GO as the working teaser plan, approved by the owner on October 13, 2026. Art direction and asset development await the owner's anime references. Visual details will be revised to match the selected direction.

## Creative intent

Let viewers feel the distance between two lives, then the possibility of closeness. The proposed teaser lasts 26 seconds in 9:16 and uses five shots. It introduces the world and the protagonists before offering a restrained mystery. It does not summarize the plot or depict Kael's awakening.

All character situations, setting details, and the anomaly below remain proposals. The last two shots can function as a mood vignette rather than a fixed event in the series timeline until their context is approved.

## Shot sequence

| Shot | Edit time | Length | Image and camera | Emotional purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 001 The Divided Kingdom | 0–6 seconds | 6 seconds | Sunrise reveals the academy's suspended terraces above a river city. Slow forward movement; foreground stone creates gentle parallax. | Wonder, followed by awareness of unequal access. |
| 002 Aurelia | 6–11 seconds | 5 seconds | Medium close-up on an academy terrace. Her attention turns from a demanding task toward the city below. Nearly still camera. | A capable person with curiosity and an interior life. |
| 003 Kael | 11–16 seconds | 5 seconds | Medium close-up at a lower-city work surface. He completes one small practical task, then glances toward something nearby with a hint of humor. | Competence and warmth before any magic. |
| 004 A Small Encounter | 16–22 seconds | 6 seconds | At a shared threshold, a windblown folded page catches against a railing. Kael steadies it; Aurelia meets his glance. A small mutual smile follows. One restrained two-shot. | Attraction begins through attention and an ordinary moment. |
| 005 An Unexplained Echo | 22–26 seconds | 4 seconds | Close view of nearby still water. A faint second ripple appears without a visible cause. Cut to black or add the working title during the final second. | Leave one question after the human connection. |

Shots 002 and 003 are parallel introductions, not an eyeline exchange across the city. Shot 004 brings both people into one space. The page is a simple proposed prop; its content is not readable and it carries no decided lore. If a two-character interaction proves unstable, simplify it to a stationary shared glance with the page already caught on the railing.

The ripple is a possible tease, not an established magical rule or confirmed awakening. A restrained sound echo can replace it if the imagery makes the implication too explicit. Neither version shows Aurelia sending power into Kael.

## Timing and sound

Generate 6-second sources for Shots 001–004 and trim Shots 002 and 003 to five seconds. Generate a 4-second source for Shot 005. The proposed sources total 28 seconds; the edit totals 26 seconds. The working title overlays the final second or replaces it, so it does not add a sixth duration block.

Begin with wind and a distant city tone. Introduce a sparse original musical phrase, leaving space for the shared smile. Resolve the final sound incompletely. No narration, exposition, or spoken dialogue is needed for the first teaser.

Keep generated sound separate from the final sound plan where possible. Use owned, original, or appropriately licensed audio with provenance. Create the title in editing so spelling and typography remain controllable.

## Production order

Approve Shot 001 direction, select its start frame, then animate and review it. Establish protagonist reference sheets and the shared location before attempting later shots. Choose the encounter and final hint before storyboarding them in detail.

The first shot should establish a reusable visual standard. A completed teaser also needs stable character identity, deliberate emotional timing, and a verified vertical export.
