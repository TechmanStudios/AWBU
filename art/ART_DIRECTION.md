# A World Between Us Proposed Art Direction

## Visual identity

Create a kingdom of suspended stone, river terraces, and deliberate gaps between structures. Paired curves separated by an interval can recur in arches, compositions, and later two-person framing. Surviving but misunderstood symbols of Resonance are now an established story premise. These particular curves are a candidate design; their exact geometry and meaning remain under review. A symbol's presence does not establish that the architecture operates through Resonance.

Use detailed painted environments with clear silhouettes, expressive anime characters, refined cel shading, and atmospheric depth. Keep invented architecture coherent enough to draw again. Concentrate magical detail at structural points rather than covering the image with particles.

## Light and color

The same sunrise reaches the academy and the streets below. Pale stone and restrained violet accents distinguish institutional magic; clay, copper, river blue, and amber emphasize the texture of ordinary life. Do not desaturate the lower city into misery or turn the upper city into unqualified goodness.

Suggested swatches for experiments: dawn gold `#EBC88A`, academy ivory `#E5DFD4`, restrained violet `#8B7CC2`, river blue `#5D8996`, clay rose `#B68170`, and deep slate `#38465C`. These are starting samples, not approved grading values.

## Architecture and access

Above: a stone academy crowned by one recognizable suspended ring and a small number of supported or floating terraces. Elegant short routes suggest effortless movement.

Below: stepped streets and roofs following the river valley. Useful, well-kept spaces convey skill, community, and care. Longer routes make unequal access visible. Specific magical transit, admission policies, and engineering explanations remain undecided.

For Shot 001, choose three landmarks that can remain stable: the main academy tower, the suspended ring, and the river bend. Avoid a skyline crowded with unrelated castles or impossible overlapping bridges.

An old ornamental feature may eventually carry a surviving symbol. Choose its design and placement before embedding it in a reusable frame. At first it should read as ordinary stonework, with no glow or activation. A later closer view can make its geometry legible. Follow [Resonance and Surviving Symbols](../development/RESONANCE_AND_SURVIVING_SYMBOLS.md) for the story boundaries.

## Camera and vertical composition

Compose natively for 9:16. Use the height of the image to express separation, while a river and a shared light source connect the frame. Keep enough lower-city detail visible that the academy does not consume the entire image.

Favor one slow forward move with slight lateral parallax. A wide, distant viewpoint limits the amount of unseen architecture the model must invent. No orbit, rapid ascent, or dramatic zoom is needed for the first shot.

## Character and magic direction

Later character tests should explore the brief's provisional appearances while prioritizing expressions, silhouette, and repeatable clothing. Approve turnarounds, expression ranges, and key costume details before producing protagonist clips.

Ordinary magic and Resonance eventually need distinguishable visual languages. For now, keep institutional effects geometric and restrained. Any teaser anomaly remains ambiguous and does not finalize a Resonance color, shape, or mechanism.

## Shot 001 look approval

Review architectural identity, shared sunrise, dignity of the lower city, readability on a phone, and restrained effects. After direction approval, develop a small number of experimental frames using the same written composition. Select one frame before spending effort on its animation.
